Regenerate both complete binder review copies, previews, reports and standalone
review booklets from the unchanged November 2025 source workbook:

python tools/build_till_binders.py \
  --binder "/path/to/Complete Till Binder - Nov 2025.xlsx" \
  --output "/path/to/empty/output-directory" \
  --verify-print --booklets

The original binder is an external input and is not included in this repository.
Use a new empty output directory when regenerating. See README.md for dependencies
and details. These outputs are review copies, not approved for operational use.
The owner-provided duplicate PLU classifications are separately maintained in
current/review-booklets/DUPLICATE_PLU_CLASSIFICATIONS.csv and its companion
review note. They annotate recommendations only; generated PLU descriptions and
duplicate records remain exactly as provided by PickList.xml.
